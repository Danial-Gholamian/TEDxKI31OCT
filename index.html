About TEDxKI

A stage for ideas from Karolinska Institutet

TEDxKI brings researchers, clinicians, students and storytellers from the Karolinska Institutet community to one stage, for short talks that start long conversations.

The event is planned by students and volunteers. We find and coach the speakers, build the stage, and film every talk for the TEDx YouTube channel so the ideas travel far beyond Solna.

What is TEDx?

x = independently organized TED event

In the spirit of discovering and spreading ideas, TED has created a program called TEDx. TEDx is a program of local, self-organized events that bring people together to share a TED-like experience. Our event is called TEDxKI, where x = independently organized TED event. At our TEDxKI event, TED Talks video and live speakers will combine to spark deep discussion and connection in a small group. Speakers never pay to join a TEDx event. Consideration, speaker coaching and event participation along with attendance are all provided free of charge. The TED Conference provides general guidance for the TEDx program, but individual TEDx events, including ours, are self-organized.
